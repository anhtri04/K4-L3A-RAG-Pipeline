# Football corpus sources

This corpus covers association football governance, club finance, player safety,
women's football, refereeing, performance analytics, talent development, and
injury forecasting. Sources were retrieved on 2026-09-20.

## Licence and attribution

- UK government material contains public sector information licensed under the
  [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/),
  except where otherwise stated. Official PDF notices are retained unchanged.
- PLOS ONE material is licensed under the
  [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
  Each JSON record retains the title, author attribution, source URL, licence,
  and a description of the normalization performed.
- Images and website navigation were not copied into the JSON records. Logos,
  coats of arms, and identified third-party material are not offered for reuse
  under the licences above.
